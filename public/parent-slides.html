A five-minute reading check.

How many words are read correctly.

How smoothly and quickly they read.

Comprehension

How much of the story they understand.

Reading the report

Two levels, and why they differ.

Your child's report shows two numbers. Knowing the difference helps you choose books, and saves any worry.

Reading level

The overall picture: how accurately, how smoothly, and with how much understanding your child read.

Accuracy level

Only how many words were read correctly in one passage. A book can be a little hard yet still be a good fit.

The two can differ, and that is perfectly normal.

School & home, together

Small things at home, big difference.

  • ✓
    Read together every day

    Even ten minutes builds the habit and the joy.

  • ✓
    Let them sound it out

    Pause before you help, give them a moment to try.

  • ✓
    Celebrate the effort

    Praise having a go, not only getting it right.

  • ✓
    Talk about the story

    Ask who, what, and why, then have them retell it.

  • ✓
    Use the app a few times a week

    Short, regular practice beats long, rare sessions.

  • ✓
    Keep the sounds firm

    Say “mmm”, not “muh”, so blending stays clean.
