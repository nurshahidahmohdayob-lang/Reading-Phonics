A learn-to-read app

A friendly app that guides your child from their very first letter sounds all the way to reading whole storybooks aloud, using the same structured, step-by-step method we use in class. It starts by finding your child's reading level, then guides them with ten engaging tools, from sounds and blending right through to reading whole stories. Best of all, it listens as your child reads and cheers them on.

Why it helps your child

Step by step, no gaps

Follows a proven phonics order, so every skill builds on the one before.

The right sounds

Children hear each letter's true sound, with actions and songs to remember it.

It listens & encourages

Your child reads aloud and the app gently checks, building real confidence.

Grows with them

Levelled stories from Year 1 to Year 6, always just the right challenge.

A clear reading level

A quick check gives you a reading level and a printable report to keep.

Screen time that teaches

Engaging, self-checking games keep children motivated and learning, not just watching.
