A learn-to-read app

A friendly app that guides your child from their very first letter sounds all the way to reading whole storybooks aloud — the same structured, step-by-step method we use in class. Ten playful tools take them, in order, from sounds → blending → spelling → reading. Best of all, it listens as your child reads and cheers them on.

Why it helps your child

Step by step, no gaps

Follows a proven phonics order, so nothing important is skipped.

The right sounds

Children hear each letter's true sound, with actions and songs to remember it.

It listens & encourages

Your child reads aloud and the app gently checks, building real confidence.

Grows with them

Levelled stories from Year 1 to Year 6 — always just the right challenge.

A clear reading level

A quick check gives you a reading level and a printable report to keep.

Screen time that teaches

Playful, self-checking games keep children motivated — learning, not just watching.
